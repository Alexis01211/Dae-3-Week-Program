// purpose

WHY THIS
EXISTS

This is a personal project built out of a deep love for gaming and its history. The goal is to showcase video games and explore how each era — from the arcades of the 70s to the modern era — changed how we play, think, and connect.

Video games aren't just entertainment. They're culture, art, and technology colliding in real time.

// key moments

GAMING THROUGH
THE DECADES

1972

The Arcade Era Begins

Pong launches and ignites a global obsession with interactive entertainment.

1977

Home Consoles Arrive

The Atari 2600 brings the arcade into living rooms across America.

1985

Nintendo Saves the Industry

After the crash of 1983, the NES revives gaming and introduces Super Mario Bros.

1996

The 3D Revolution

Super Mario 64 and the PlayStation define how we think about 3D space and storytelling.

2000s–Now

Online & Beyond

Multiplayer goes global. Indie games rise. Mobile changes everything. Again.
